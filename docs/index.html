The idea in one breath

Hyperbolic quantum LDPC codes store many logical qubits per physical qubit, at low distance. Fine-graining the tiling flattens it and buys distance, at quadratic qubit cost. Existing constructions freeze that trade at design time. A breathing code makes it a schedule: the block inhales (a quad refinement, with borrowed qubits from a reservoir) into a flat high-distance phase for a compute window or a noise burst, then exhales the qubits back into dense curved storage. Both directions are code deformation with one deterministic fold detector per refined face, so decoding runs through the morph, and the number of logical qubits is pinned by topology the whole time.

What the paper shows

One cycle vs static holds

Fixed morph overhead, then near-free holding at d = 6. Break-even 14–20 rounds at p = 1–2%; at T = 48 one cycle cuts block error 2.3× (torus) and 2.5× (Bring).

Circuit level, p = 10−3

The structure survives explicit CNOT extraction and strengthens: break-even ≈10 rounds; T = 48 wins grow to 3.7× (torus) and 3.8× (Bring).

Versus the zoned baseline

Teleport wins the hop budget; breathing protects the whole block with 180 extra atoms versus 568, a 3.2× footprint advantage.

Seams and graded distance

Transport concentrates on tile seams (66.5% of checks transport-free at ℓ = 8, perimeter not area scaling); inflating c transversal columns raises a targeted logical to d + c, exactly.

Exhale to operate

Deflating one operator corridor restores coarse operator weight for the targeted logical (6 to 3, ILP-exact), guaranteed by a min-max duality; collateral exposure is its price on curved codes.

Honest scope

Where breathing wins

  • Whole-block protection (noise bursts, scheduled danger windows) at roughly a third of the zoned architecture's peak atom footprint.
  • Short protection windows, below the patch bank's surgery amortization.
  • Graded, per-logical, in-place distance: d rises to d + c per inflated transversal column, a capability the zoned design does not have.
  • Cheap in-place logical operations: a corridor exhale provably restores coarse operator weight (6 to 3) exactly where and when it is needed.

Where it loses, and what it requires

  • Long single-logical holds: teleporting to a static patch costs less transport, essentially always.
  • Gate schedules must be certified jointly across coarse, fine, and morph phases; 5 of 8 otherwise good schedules on the curved code hide a mid-morph fault-distance drop (3 to 2).
  • Needs hardware with mid-run atom rearrangement (reconfigurable neutral-atom arrays are the natural host).
  • No benefit above the pseudo-threshold crossing: on ibm_kingston the never-morphed d = 6 code also lost to d = 3, and the morph checks failed exactly there.
  • Hardware validation so far covers the one-dimensional morph primitive only; the 2D curved codes do not fit current superconducting connectivity.

Abstract

Show the abstract

Hyperbolic quantum LDPC codes trade protection for storage density: many logical qubits per physical qubit at logarithmic distance, while fine-graining flattens the tiling and buys distance at quadratic qubit cost. In existing constructions this curvature is fixed at design time. We introduce breathing codes: one quantum LDPC block whose fine-graining level is a runtime variable, morphed fault-tolerantly between a dense curved storage phase and a flatter high-distance compute phase. We analyze it end to end, phenomenologically and at circuit level with explicit CNOT extraction, on a flat control (toric, [[18,2,3]] to [[72,2,6]]) and the hyperbolic Bring code ([[30,8,3]] to [[120,8,6]]). Phenomenologically the exact fault distance of the full breathing circuit equals the smaller of the coarse and fine distances. At circuit level it becomes schedule dependent: hook faults reduce it from 3 to 2 mid-morph in five of eight otherwise good schedules on the curved code; morph-safe schedules exist and certify exactly in seconds, a compile-time rule. The break-even hold time is 14-20 rounds phenomenologically at p=1-2% and about 10 rounds at circuit level (p=0.001), beyond which one cycle cuts logical block error 2.3-3.8x at T=48. Against the zoned teleport architecture, breathing pays more transport for long single-logical holds but protects a whole block with 3.2x fewer atoms. Two IBM devices validate the one-dimensional morph primitive, with coherent logical transport through a full cycle and a break-even of at most 8 rounds. In reverse, the same machinery lowers the minimum operator weight of a targeted logical back to the coarse distance (6 to 3, ILP-exact), guaranteed by a min-max duality the inflation direction lacks. Breathing codes recast error protection as a schedulable runtime resource rather than an architectural constant.
